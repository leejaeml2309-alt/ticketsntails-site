Privacy & Cookies

Last updated 20 November 2026.

Tickets & Tails is a travel journal published at ticketsntails.com, alongside PetPolicyCheck. This page explains what this site collects, the cookies it uses, and how you can choose.

What this site collects

These pages have no accounts, no comment forms and no newsletter signup. We do not ask you to create a login or to send a name or payment detail through this site.

If you write to us, we will receive whatever you include in that message, and we will use it only to reply.

The servers that host the site (GitHub Pages) receive standard technical request data such as IP address, browser type and the page asked for, in order to deliver the pages. We do not run our own analytics unless you allow analytics cookies below.

Cookies and Google Consent Mode

When the site first loads, advertising and analytics cookies are set to denied through Google Consent Mode v2 (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization and analytics_storage). Ads may still appear in a non-personalised mode until you accept. Your choice is stored in your browser (a local preference, not a tracking cookie of our own) so we can remember it on later visits.

You can Accept, Reject, or open Settings to allow advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or both. You can change that choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer, or the button below.

Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-7693501377730690) to show advertisements. Google may set or read cookies and use device identifiers to serve and measure ads, according to the choice you make. Until you accept, AdSense is asked to request non-personalised ads. Google’s own explanation is in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

For readers in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires a Google-certified consent management platform. The banner on this site implements Consent Mode v2; the AdSense “Privacy & messaging” GDPR message (a Google-certified CMP) should also be enabled on the publisher account for those regions.

Amazon Associates

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Some pages may include links to Amazon. Those links use a tracking tag so Amazon can credit a referral if you buy something. Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored nofollow noopener". Amazon’s privacy practices are described in the Amazon Privacy Notice.

How long choices last

Your cookie preference stays in your browser until you clear site data or change it here. Advertising and analytics cookies set by Google follow Google’s own retention periods.

Children

This journal is written for adults planning travel with a dog or a cat. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this notice changes in a material way, we will update the date at the top of the page.

Contact

Questions about privacy on this journal can be sent through the contact page.
