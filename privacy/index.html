Privacy

What happens to your data on ticketsntails.com, in plain words.

Last updated November 28, 2026.

The short version

Pawpartout · Life Design & Legacy has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no ads. We do not ask for your name, email or payment details anywhere on ticketsntails.com.

Hosting

The site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Analytics and cookies

Every page uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-K45B2WNXMC) to count visits. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies, such as _ga, to tell visits apart. It is the only tag on this site: there is no advertising and there are no advertising cookies. Google’s handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your browser also remembers, on this device only, that you closed the cookie notice or the Studio’s small-screen notice. The 3D Pet-Interior Studio does not save your room: it stays in the open browser tab, and nothing is sent to us. Fonts are served from ticketsntails.com itself.

Affiliate links

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. The Affiliate disclosure page explains which links are affiliate links.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites, such as veterinary sources, PetPolicyCheck and pawpartout.com. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
