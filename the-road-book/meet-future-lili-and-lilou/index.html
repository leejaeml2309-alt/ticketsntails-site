Meet Future, Lili and Lilou

Published 21 November 2026

This journal begins with three animals, and with a move that has not yet been made.

Lili, a tortoiseshell, and Lilou, a black-and-white cat, were born in July 2016. They were part of a litter of four sisters — two tortoiseshell and two black-and-white — curled up in pairs in the cage. They came from a pet shop in the Paris region. Marc brought them with him to Korea at the end of 2016.

Future is a Shih Tzu, adopted in 2024 in the Daegu region of South Korea. He is officially eight years old, though Marc thinks he is more like six or seven, given how energetic he is.

The family is planning a move from Korea to Texas.

The drawings on this site are linocuts of the household, made from their own photographs.

Tickets & Tails will follow that journey as a travel story: the road book of a mixed household, the official texts that apply when a dog and cats travel together, checklists that can be printed and updated, and camping read against park rules. It will not replace those official texts. When a rule matters, we will point to the government or airline page that states it, and to PetPolicyCheck when a current official summary is needed.

The first of those practical notes are already in the journal: a Korea-to-U.S. checklist, a reading of National Park Service and Texas State Parks rules for camping with a dog, and a note on what official rules treat differently when dogs and cats share an itinerary.

For the full, current official rules, see PetPolicyCheck’s United States page and the South Korea page.
